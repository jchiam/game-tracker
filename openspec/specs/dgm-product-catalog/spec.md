# dgm-product-catalog Specification

## Purpose

The seed-generated Digimon product catalog: products (one per release) with their colourway / regional variants, the per-product progress guides, the generator and its validation, and the Wikimon drift report that informs the seed without ever writing it.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Product catalog data file

The system SHALL maintain a static catalog of Digimon digivice / virtual-pet **products**, each with its **variants** (colourways and regional releases), in `src/data/digimon/products.ts`, generated by `scripts/update-dgm-data.mjs` with the shared generated-file banner, exporting `DgmVariant`, `DgmProgressItem`, `DgmProgressGroup`, `DgmProgressTrack`, `DgmProgressGuide`, `DgmProduct`, `const ALL_PRODUCTS: DgmProduct[]`, and `const DGM_LINES: string[]`.

Each `DgmProduct` SHALL have `id` (kebab-case slug, unique), `name`, `line` (open string), `series` (open string), `releaseYear` (the earliest variant year), `variants` (non-empty, ordered), and optional `guide`. Each `DgmVariant` SHALL have `id` (kebab-case slug, unique across the whole catalog), `colorway`, `releaseYear` (four-digit integer), `region` (`'JP' | 'NA' | 'EU' | 'ASIA'`), and `imageUrl` (`/assets/digimon/devices/{variantId}.webp`). `DGM_LINES` SHALL list the distinct `line` values in first-appearance order of `ALL_PRODUCTS`.

#### Scenario: Catalog accessible at runtime

- **WHEN** a component imports `ALL_PRODUCTS` and `DGM_LINES` from `@/data/digimon/products`
- **THEN** every product carries the fields above with at least one variant, variant ids are unique across products, and every `DGM_LINES` value matches at least one product's `line`

#### Scenario: Entries in seed order

- **WHEN** the catalog is regenerated
- **THEN** `ALL_PRODUCTS` order equals the product seed order, each product's `variants` order equals its seed order, and `DGM_LINES` order equals first appearance

### Requirement: Product catalog is generated from a hand-authored nested seed

`scripts/update-dgm-data.mjs` SHALL read `scripts/seeds/dgm-products.json` — an array of `{ id, name, line, series, wikimon?, variants: [{ id, colorway, releaseYear, region, imageSource, wikimon? }] }` where `imageSource` is an http(s) URL, a `wikimon:<File name>` reference resolved through the Wikimon API at run time, or `null` — and SHALL be the only writer of `products.ts`. The seed is curated by hand and is the source of truth; `src/data/digimon/**` is never hand-edited. The script SHALL compose `scripts/lib/pipeline.mjs` and SHALL upload each variant image once through `ensureAsset` to `/assets/digimon/devices/{variantId}.webp` (variant ids equal the former device ids, so no asset moves).

#### Scenario: Seed validation rejects bad products

- **WHEN** the seed contains a duplicate product id, a duplicate variant id (within or across products), a product with no variants, a missing required field, a `region` outside the enum, or a non-four-digit variant `releaseYear`
- **THEN** the script exits non-zero naming the offending product or variant and writes nothing

#### Scenario: Product year derived from variants

- **WHEN** a product's variants carry years 2019 and 2017
- **THEN** the generated product `releaseYear` is 2017

#### Scenario: Failed image does not block generation

- **WHEN** a variant's `imageSource` cannot be downloaded
- **THEN** the variant is still emitted with its local `imageUrl`, the failure is listed in the missing-assets summary, and the exit code follows the shared pipeline convention

### Requirement: Progress guides are hand-written per product

`scripts/seeds/dgm-guides/<productId>.json` — `{ overall: 'track-mean' | 'item-weighted', tracks: [{ id, label, groups: [{ id, label, items: [{ id, label, hint?, requires? }] }] }] }` — SHALL be attached by the generator as that product's `guide`. Item ids SHALL be `{trackId}:{slug}` and unique within the guide; every `requires` id SHALL name an item of the same guide. Guides are written once per product from the device and its manual; third-party guides (Humulos) MAY inform the transcription but SHALL NOT be fetched by any script. The generated banner SHALL name the references.

#### Scenario: Guide attached to its product

- **WHEN** `scripts/seeds/dgm-guides/dv-25th-color-evolution.json` exists and the product seed has `dv-25th-color-evolution`
- **THEN** `ALL_PRODUCTS.find(p => p.id === 'dv-25th-color-evolution').guide` carries its tracks in file order

#### Scenario: Guide validation rejects bad files

- **WHEN** a guide file names no product, has an item id not prefixed by its track id, a duplicate item id, a `requires` id that does not resolve, an `overall` outside the enum, or no item at all
- **THEN** the script exits non-zero naming the file and the offending id and writes nothing

#### Scenario: Digivice -25th COLOR EVOLUTION- guide

- **WHEN** the `dv-25th-color-evolution` guide is read
- **THEN** it has `overall 'track-mean'` and exactly two tracks in order: `partners` and `map` (one group per map; each cleared location is followed immediately by its secret-area item — labelled `{Location} · secret area`, hinted `Secret · {Digimon}`, no `requires` — and locations chain through `requires`); there is no Friends track; the Omegamon item requires WarGreymon, MetalGarurumon, and `map:subspace`

#### Scenario: Secret area follows its location

- **WHEN** the File Island group of the `map` track is read
- **THEN** its items run Tropical Jungle, Tropical Jungle · secret area, Lake, Lake · secret area, … and the Digital Forest location (which has no secret area) is followed directly by the next location

### Requirement: External sources inform the seed, never overwrite it

No external source SHALL write `scripts/seeds/dgm-products.json` or any guide. `scripts/update-dgm-data.mjs --sync-wikimon` SHALL fetch every Wikimon page named by a product's `wikimon` plus Wikimon's `List of Virtual Pets`, and print per product: variant colourways the seed lacks, variant years the page does not list, version titles with no variant, gallery image candidates for variants whose `imageSource` is `null`, and finally the list pages no product references. It SHALL NOT modify any seed or generated file. CC BY-SA content SHALL be attributed in the banner; Humulos SHALL NOT be a catalog source.

#### Scenario: Sync reports drift only

- **WHEN** `--sync-wikimon` runs and a product page lists a colourway with no variant
- **THEN** the script prints it under that product's heading and exits without writing

### Requirement: Manual-dispatch regeneration workflow

`.github/workflows/update-dgm-data.yml` SHALL stay `workflow_dispatch` only and open a pull request with the regenerated `products.ts` when it changed.

#### Scenario: Dispatch opens a regeneration PR

- **WHEN** the workflow is dispatched after a seed change
- **THEN** it runs `scripts/update-dgm-data.mjs` and opens a PR if `products.ts` changed
