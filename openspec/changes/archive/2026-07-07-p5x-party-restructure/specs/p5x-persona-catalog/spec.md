## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: P5X persona catalog

The system SHALL provide a static catalog of equippable personas at `src/data/persona-5-phantom-x/personas.ts` (`ALL_PERSONAS`), generated by the P5X update script from Prydwen — never hand-edited (`src/data/**` is generated). Each persona SHALL expose `id`, `name`, `rarity`, `role`, `element`, and `imageUrl` (a local `/assets/persona-5-phantom-x/personas/{id}.webp` path resolved to the ImageKit CDN at render time).

#### Scenario: Catalog generated from source

- **WHEN** `node scripts/update-p5x-data.mjs` runs
- **THEN** `personas.ts` is regenerated from the Prydwen personas page-data (metadata) and rendered grid (image URLs), with each persona icon uploaded to ImageKit; the file is never edited by hand

#### Scenario: Personas selectable in the party editor

- **WHEN** a P5X party's persona slot (index 1–3) picker opens
- **THEN** it lists entries from `ALL_PERSONAS` (the union tagged `entityType: 'persona'`), searchable by name, excluding personas already assigned to another slot

#### Scenario: Personas are not roster-tracked

- **WHEN** the P5X roster view renders
- **THEN** personas do not appear as individually owned/tracked entities — they exist only as party-composition equipment for Wonder
